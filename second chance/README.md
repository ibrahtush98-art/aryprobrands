## 🗂️ Categorized Asset Subfolders

- **`logos/`**: Vector SVGs and high-resolution transparent PNGs for both dark and light backgrounds, including AP/PM monograms and favicons.
- **`showcase/`**: High-resolution photography (Branding, Cinema Video Production, Web & Tech Laptop Mockup, Executive Team, Office Studio, and SVG Web Mockup).
- **`merchandise/`**: Real-world brand application mockups (Apparel hoodie, Business cards, 3D Wall sign, Ceramic mug, Embroidered cap).
- **`brand_guide_slices/`**: Modular slices 0–9 from the official brand identity guideline deck.
- **`styles/`**: Custom CSS design system (`promedia-theme.css`).

*Note: All individual asset files are also copied directly into the root of `second chance/` for fast drag-and-drop or batch selection.*
